Fare Frenzy

An arcade taxi rush. Grab fares, race the clock, and pull off stunts for tips in a city full of traffic.

  • 🚕Stop in a customer's ring, follow the arrow, and drop them off before their timer runs out.
  • ⏱️Speedy drop-offs add time to your shift clock, so a great run keeps going.
  • 🌀Near misses, big air and drifts earn tips, and stunts chained together build a combo up to x10.
  • 🏆Finish your shift with a grade from S to E and chase your best score.

In private testing now. Public release coming soon.

Sprout Isle

A cozy island garden. Plant, water and harvest crops that grow in real time, even while you're away.

  • 🌱Tap soil to plant, water to grow twice as fast, and harvest when your crop sparkles.
  • ✨Chase rare Golden and Rainbow harvests worth many times the usual price.
  • 🧺Sell at the Market Stall, restock at the Seed Shed, and grow your garden plot.
  • 🌧️Rain showers, a daily Hot Crop and a daily gift streak give you a reason to come back.

Early prototype. Follow along for updates.
